📋 Official Rulebook & Events 📋

THE GAMES & EVENTS

Study the rules, prepare your excuses, and review the scoring criteria for absolute glory.

⚖️ General Scoring & Chaos Rules

Standard Events Points scale based on placement (e.g., 8 pts for 1st, 7 for 2nd, etc.). DNP (Did Not Participate) results in 0 points.
Duck Hunt Points awarded equal exactly the number of hidden resin ducks you successfully find around the house!
City Selfies & Special Custom team points: 1st place = 10 pts, 2nd = 7 pts, 3rd = 4 pts, last = 1 pt.
Event #1 🦆

Duck Hunt

Hunt mini resin ducks hidden strategically around the house. Your score is equal to the total number of ducks you find!

Event #2 📸

City Selfies

A treasure hunt around the city. Take selfies with a list of items and rush back to the finish line. Custom podium points apply!

Event #3 🔓

Crack the Code

Online escape room challenge. Final task requires you to sprint and bring me something from the house beginning with a certain letter.

Event #4 ⛳

Golf Putting

Put the most golf balls successfully onto the green to claim putting supremacy.

Event #5 🏓

Padel Pong

Use a Padel Racket to hit a ball accurately into a designated bin or bucket.

Event #6 🎾

Sticky Bounce

Bounce a tennis ball so it lands and successfully sticks on the velcro target.

Event #7 💍

Ring Toss

Score the most points by throwing rings onto assigned targets.

Event #8 🛍️

Bean Bag Toss

Throw the bean bags into the nets with precision and poise.

Event #9 ✨

The Entrance

Each player walks out and performs an entrance. Everyone else marks it, determining the official event order!

Event #10 🃏

Cut the Deck

Knockout round! Cut a card from the deck. Lowest card is eliminated. Continue until 1 winner remains.

Event #11 🍻

Beer Pong

Most table tennis balls successfully landed in the cups wins.

Event #12 🏃

The Ultimate Relay Race

Leg 1: Sorting Socks. Leg 2: Ping Pong ball in hot tub (no hands). Leg 3: Cup stack on table. Leg 4: Shoot a target with the Nerf gun to finish!
