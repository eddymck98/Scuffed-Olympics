Scoring Protocol & Rules

🎯 Standard Events

Scored by placement: 7th gets 10 points, 1st gets 8 points (with scaling positions in between). DNP means 0 points. May the best athlete survive!

🦆 Duck Hunt

Special scoring: The total number of mini resin ducks you successfully find equals your exact point total! Every duck counts toward glory.

🗺️ City Treasure Hunt

Custom pods: 1st team gets 10 points, 2nd gets 7 points, 3rd gets 4 points, and last gets 1 point. Capture proofs and conquer the checklist!
